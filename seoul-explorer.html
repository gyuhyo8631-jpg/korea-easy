Is My Taxi Fare OK?

Use this as a reference before riding. Airport international-taxi section fares use Seoul's official zone table. Seoul-to-Seoul estimates are rough meter-based ranges, not quotations.

Fare safety: confirm the meter or approved fixed fare before departure. Tolls, traffic, late-night and out-of-Seoul surcharges can change the final amount. Keep the receipt and vehicle number if you need to question a fare.

Official Seoul Taxi Fares

Official sedan-taxi meter reference: ₩4,800 up to 1.6 km; then ₩100/131 m and timed fare when moving slowly. Late-night surcharge: +20% (22:00–23:00), +40% (23:00–02:00), +20% (02:00–04:00). International Taxi Incheon Airport ↔ Seoul section fares vary by zone.
