T-Money Card Guide for Foreigners

A practical introduction to using T-money and choosing a transportation card while traveling in Korea.

Updated August 2026 · KOREA EASY

Quick answer
T-money is a rechargeable transportation card widely used on public transportation in Korea. For a first-time visitor, it is a simple way to tap in and out on buses and subways without buying a separate ticket for every ride.

What can you use it for?

T-money is primarily useful for buses and subways, and T-money materials also describe use across taxis and other participating services. Availability can vary by service, so check signage when using it outside public transit.

Where should a visitor get one?

Tourist-oriented transportation cards and standard prepaid transportation cards are commonly available through convenience stores and transport-related sales locations. Korea Tourism Organization also publishes a transportation-card guide for visitors.

How do you use it?

Load value onto the card, tap the reader when entering public transportation, and remember to tap when leaving where required. Keeping a modest balance topped up is easier than repeatedly buying single-use tickets.
