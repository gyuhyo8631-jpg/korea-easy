RECOMMENDED ROUTE

AREX + Seoul Subway Line 4

This is usually the easiest public-transport route for first-time visitors.

1

Arrive at T1 or T2

Finish immigration, baggage claim and customs.
2

Take AREX

Follow Airport Railroad / AREX signs toward Seoul Station.
3

Transfer at Seoul Station

Change to Seoul Subway Line 4 toward Myeongdong.
4

Arrive at Myeongdong

Check your hotel or destination before choosing the exit.

Which option should you choose?

Recommended

AREX + Subway

Good for most solo travelers and couples with manageable luggage.

Airport Bus

Taxi

Most convenient

TERMINAL GUIDE

Terminal 1 vs Terminal 2

Incheon Airport Terminal 1

After entering the public arrivals area, follow signs for Airport Railroad / AREX. If you need a T-money card, check airport convenience stores or official transport facilities.

Incheon Airport Terminal 2

Terminal 2 also connects directly to AREX. Follow Airport Railroad signs after leaving arrivals. Allow extra time to purchase or top up a transport card if needed.

Airport shop locations, fares and operating details can change. Always confirm current information using airport signs and official transport information before travel.
T-MONEY

Do you need T-money?

T-money is useful for Seoul subway and bus travel. If you plan to use regular public transport after arriving in Korea, it is convenient to prepare a card before heading into Seoul.

  • Useful on Seoul subway and buses
  • Can reduce the need to buy individual tickets repeatedly
  • Keep some Korean won cash available for top-up situations

MYEONGDONG STATION

Which exit should you use?

Exit 6

Commonly used for the main Myeongdong shopping area and central streets.

Exit 8 / 9

Useful for different hotel, restaurant and shopping directions around Myeongdong.
The best exit depends on your exact hotel or destination. Check the map before leaving the station, especially if you have luggage.

Korean noodles, BBQ, street food, cafes and traditional restaurants.

Shopping

Beauty stores, fashion, souvenirs and large retail areas.

Nearby sights

Namsan, N Seoul Tower, Namdaemun and central Seoul attractions are nearby.
