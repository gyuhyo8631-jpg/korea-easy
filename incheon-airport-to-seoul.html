Incheon Airport to Seoul

Compare AREX, airport bus and taxi options for getting from Incheon International Airport to Seoul.

Updated August 2026 · KOREA EASY

Quick answer
For many travelers, AREX is the easiest route to Seoul Station. Airport buses can be better when they stop close to your hotel, especially with luggage. A taxi is the most direct but usually costs more.

AREX: fast and predictable

Seoul's official visitor information describes two airport-rail options. The all-stop train takes about 59 minutes from Terminal 1 and 66 minutes from Terminal 2 to Seoul Station, while the express train takes about 43 minutes from Terminal 1 and 51 minutes from Terminal 2.

Airport bus

Incheon Airport Service publishes current Seoul, regional, Incheon and late-night bus timetables. Tickets can be purchased at ticket offices and unmanned ticket machines. Because schedules can change, check the latest timetable on the day you travel.

Which should you choose?

Seoul Station / light luggage: AREX is usually straightforward. Hotel near a limousine stop / heavy luggage: check the airport bus first. Several travelers or late arrival: compare a taxi with the remaining public-transport options.

KOREA EASY tip: Save your hotel's Korean name and address before leaving the airport. It helps with maps, bus-stop confirmation and taxis.

Before leaving the terminal

Confirm your terminal, current operating time, last departure and hotel location. Incheon Airport bus schedules explicitly warn that operating times can change, so avoid relying on an old screenshot.
