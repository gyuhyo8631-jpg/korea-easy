INCHEON AIRPORT · T1 & T2

Find the right curb, before you leave arrivals.

A terminal-by-terminal orientation guide for airport taxis, Seoul and regional buses, and AREX airport rail.

Gate and route areas are based on Incheon Airport guidance. Bus timetables change, so open the official route search before boarding.

Your arrival sequence

1Confirm whether your flight arrives at T1 or T2.
2Use the correct floor and exit zone for taxi, bus or rail.
3Check the live official bus route before you queue.
Quick answerAREX is in the Transportation Center B1 at both terminals. For buses, T1 uses curbside exit zones on 1F, while T2 uses the B1 Transportation Center for Seoul, Gyeonggi and regional buses. Taxis use 1F at both terminals.

AREX AIRPORT RAIL

Both terminals: take the signs to Transportation Center B1.

AREX connects T2 to T1 in about 6 minutes and continues to key Seoul transfer stations. Choose all-stop or express after checking your destination and the current timetable.
